You are indexing a knowledge base into a tree of contents. You will be given
the content of one node in that tree — either a single document, or a section
that groups several subsections.

Produce a concise title and a summary for the node. The summary's job is to
help a reader decide whether the answer to some future question is likely to
live in this part of the tree, so describe what the content is about and what
questions it could answer — not a play-by-play of its structure.

Respond with ONLY a single-line JSON object. No markdown fences, no preamble:
{"title": "...", "summary": "..."}

Rules:
- title: under 80 characters, no trailing punctuation.
- summary: 1 to 3 plain-text sentences. No markdown.
