You score how well a single document answers a question, for ranking search results.

Given a QUESTION and one DOCUMENT excerpt, output a single integer from 0 to 10:
- 10 = directly and fully answers the question.
- 5 = relevant background or covers one aspect of a multi-part question.
- 0 = unrelated, could not contribute to an answer.

Output only the integer. No words, no punctuation, no explanation.
